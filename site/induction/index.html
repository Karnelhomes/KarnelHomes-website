⚠️ Site Safety Rules

1. PPE Requirements: High-visibility clothing, steel-capped boots, and hard hats must be worn at all times while on site.

2. Incidents & Hazards: All injuries, near-misses, and site hazards must be reported to the Site Supervisor immediately.

3. Housekeeping: Keep your work area clean and free of trip hazards. Dispose of all rubbish in the designated site bins before leaving.

4. Drugs & Alcohol: Zero tolerance policy. Anyone found under the influence will be removed from the site immediately.

5. Site Alterations: Do not alter scaffolding, remove edge protection, or operate machinery you are not licensed for.

6. Access: Park only in designated areas and do not block neighboring driveways or footpaths.
